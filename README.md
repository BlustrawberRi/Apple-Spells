# Apple Spells

Biskit was on his way to his new internship in another town. But upon arriving there, they found the witch of the spell and potion shop to be missing.
Mistaken as a new witch, the town's inhabitants quickly commission remedies to their rising heap of problems this winter, that doesn't seem to want to come to an end.
Biskit has to make due with the notes that are left over and the tools they find lying around the shop and figure out how to grow and care for plants, yield their produce and transform it into different kinds of small magic products fit to the needs of their customers.
